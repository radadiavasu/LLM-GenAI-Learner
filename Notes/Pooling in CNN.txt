Pooling

- There is an down sampling technique of feature map by selecting maximum and average values from an image. It means it just extract important features rather than whole image.

Advantages

- Memory efficient

- Translation Variance: It just extract the feature rather than the location of that feature for better grip in learning.

- Speed of training


Disadvantages

- Image Segmentation(Translation Variance): It works well in obj classification but fails where location of that feature matters like image segmentation.

- We may loose critical information where down sampling happens.
